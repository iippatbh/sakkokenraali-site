What the app is

Sakkokenraali is a ledger for a sports team's internal fines, bounties and squad fund. It records what your team agrees between themselves. It is not a payment service, a bank, or a party to any payment: money changes hands between team members outside the app, and the app only records what members report and approve.

Your team's rules are your team's

Fines and bounties in the app are informal arrangements between the members of a team. We don't enforce them, mediate disputes, or verify that any payment happened. The team's general or admin decides what is recorded.

Accounts and teams

You may use the app without an account (tied to your phone) or with Sign in with Apple. Teams are private; whoever holds an invite link or code can join, so share them only with your team. The team admin can remove members and regenerate the invite. You may leave a team at any time from your profile.

Price

During the launch offer the first season is free for every team. Afterwards the app offers a Season Pass: one auto-renewable subscription per team, bought through the App Store, priced as shown in the app before purchase. It renews each season unless cancelled in your Apple ID settings at least 24 hours before the period ends. Apple handles billing and refunds under its own terms.
